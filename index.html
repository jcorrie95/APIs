Apis

A private dashboard for running and monitoring a personal set of stock and ETF trading bots on a Charles Schwab brokerage account.

What it does

  • Runs the owner's trading bots on a schedule, on paper (simulated) or on the owner's own Schwab account.
  • Shows each bot's results, trades and risk limits, and asks the owner to confirm every change to real money with a PIN.
  • Writes a daily report. If the owner connects Google Drive, it saves that report as a Google Doc in a folder called "Apis for Claude" in the owner's own Drive.

Who can use it

Apis is a personal tool. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. It runs only on the owner's own computer and private server.
