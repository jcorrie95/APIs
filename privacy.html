Apis Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Apis is a personal trading dashboard used only by its owner. This policy explains what Apis does when the owner connects it to their Google account.

What Google access Apis asks for

Apis asks only for the Google Drive permission "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file). With it, Apis can see and change only the files that Apis itself created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive.

What Apis does with it

  • Creates a folder called "Apis for Claude" in your Drive.
  • Saves the dashboard's daily report and activity log there as Google Docs, and replaces them with newer versions.
  • Deletes its own reports after 45 days.

What Apis never does

  • Never reads any Drive file it did not create.
  • Never puts passwords, PINs, access keys, login tokens or full account numbers into the reports. Account numbers are cut to their last four digits.
  • Never sells, shares or sends your Google data to anyone. The reports stay in your own Drive.
  • Never uses your Google data for advertising.

Where your Google sign-in is kept

The long-term Google sign-in key that Apis receives is stored only in the owner's own Apis settings file, on the owner's own computer and private server.

How to stop it

Remove Apis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or delete the Google Drive settings from Apis. The reports already in your Drive stay until you delete them.

Use of Google data

Apis's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
